EIB GROUP
PROPOSED DEFENCE GATEWAY AI
An Indigenous AI-Powered Platform for Defence Information,
Administration and Decision Support

Introduction

The way modern defence organizations operate is changing. Military institutions today generate
and manage huge amounts of information personnel records, logistics reports, equipment
information, training records, operational documents, surveillance information, maintenance
reports and many other forms of data.

The challenge is not always the lack of information. In many cases, the challenge is being able
to find the right information quickly, understand it and use it effectively.

This is where we believe Artificial Intelligence can make a meaningful difference.

SOAIR is proposing the development of Defence Gateway AI, a secure, indigenous platform
designed to provide authorized military personnel with a single intelligent gateway to the
information and systems they already have access to.

The objective is not to replace existing military systems.

Instead, the platform would sit above existing systems and help authorized personnel find,
understand, connect and use information more efficiently.




The Idea
Imagine an authorized officer being able to log into one secure platform and simply ask:

      “Show me the equipment currently awaiting maintenance.”

Or:

      “Prepare a summary of the training activities for this command over the last
      quarter.”
Or:

           “Find the documents relating to this policy and summarize the key requirements.”

Instead of searching through different databases, folders, reports and documents, the platform
would help locate the relevant information and present it in a clear and understandable format.

This is the basic idea behind Defence Gateway AI.

It is intended to become an intelligent digital gateway for defence organizations.




What the Platform Would Cover
1. AI Defence Assistant

The heart of the platform would be an AI assistant designed specifically for approved defence
information.

It could help authorized personnel:

      ●​    Search military documents
      ●​    Find policies and procedures
      ●​    Summarize lengthy reports
      ●​    Compare documents
      ●​    Prepare administrative reports
      ●​    Extract information from large datasets
      ●​    Identify relevant records
      ●​    Answer questions based on approved information sources

The AI would also show the source of the information it used, allowing the user to verify the
answer.


2. Personnel Management

The platform could provide an easier way for authorized personnel to access administrative
information.

Depending on the user's clearance and role, this could include:

      ●​ Training records
      ●​ Qualifications
   ●​    Certifications
   ●​    Personnel administration
   ●​    Leave requests
   ●​    Posting-related administration
   ●​    Skills and competency records
   ●​    Welfare-related requests

The objective is to reduce unnecessary paperwork and make routine administration faster.




3. Logistics and Equipment

Another important part of the platform would be logistics.

Military organizations manage a large number of assets, equipment and supplies.

The system could help track:

   ●​    Equipment
   ●​    Vehicles
   ●​    Spare parts
   ●​    Inventory
   ●​    Maintenance
   ●​    Procurement
   ●​    Warehousing
   ●​    Supply requests
   ●​    Equipment lifecycle

For example, a commander could ask:

        “Which equipment requires maintenance?”

The system could produce the relevant information from the authorized logistics systems.




4. Training and Readiness

The platform could also bring training information together.

It could help management understand:

   ●​ Who has completed specific training
   ●​ Available qualifications
   ●​ Training requirements
    ●​ Expired certifications
    ●​ Training gaps
    ●​ Upcoming courses

Rather than relying on multiple spreadsheets and reports, authorized leadership could have a
clearer picture of training and personnel development.

6. AI Correlation
One of the most important features would be the ability to identify relationships between
information that may otherwise sit in different systems.

For example, the platform could analyze authorized:

   ●​   Training information
   ●​   Equipment information
   ●​   Maintenance records
   ●​   Logistics information
   ●​   Personnel information

and help leadership understand where there are recurring issues, gaps or areas that require
attention.

This is not about allowing AI to make military decisions on its own.

The purpose is to give human decision-makers better information so they can make better
decisions.
